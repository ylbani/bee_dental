## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, editorial-grade digital experience for aesthetic and clinical dentistry. It merges the meticulous cleanliness of elite medical environments with the bespoke, welcoming warmth of luxury personal care. The design evokes serenity, surgical precision, and radiant confidence, calming patient anxiety while conveying uncompromising professional excellence.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate/Modern** precision with **Editorial Elegance**:
- **Pristine Clinical Clarity:** Generous white space, immaculate layout hygiene, and sharp visual rhythm that communicate absolute sanitation and care.
- **Warm Architectural Polish:** Softened edges, refined dual-tone contrast (clinical crispness counterpointed by deep navy anchor tones and honey-warm accents), and delicate ambient shadows.
- **Editorial Typography:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with ultra-legible, humanist geometric sans-serif body copy, lending an elevated aesthetic touch reminiscent of luxury wellness publications.

## Colors

The palette balances therapeutic reassurance with premium aesthetic sophistication:

- **Primary (`#0D9488` - Pristine Mint Teal):** The foundational clinical accent. Conveys hygienic freshness, biomedical safety, and calm reassurance. Used for primary conversion triggers, active treatment indicators, and verified badges.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` - Deep Obsidian Slate):** Provides grounding structure, deep contrast, and luxury presence. Serves as high-contrast structural framing, dark narrative hero sections, and primary typography.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Honey Amber):** A subtle nod to the hive motif. Adds warmth, premium touchpoints, and highlighting for VIP aesthetic treatments, smile transformations, and ratings.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Neutral):** Provides balanced mid-tones for secondary meta-data, delicate dividing rules, and neutral borders (`#E2E8F0` / `#F8FAFC`).

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** Pure clinical pearl (`#FCFDFD`) transitioning to luminous white cards (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Subtle Surface Tint:** Cool mint-infused porcelain (`#F0FDFA`) for medical informational containers and treatment highlights.
- **Dark Inversion:** Selected premium sections (e.g., cosmetic dentistry showcases, footer zones) deploy `#0F172A` with `#0D9488` highlights for deep luxury drama.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges high-end editorial cosmetics with medical precision:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Brings a sculptured, human elegance to titles and brand statements. High stroke contrast reinforces cosmetic artistry and refinement. Use italic treatments sparingly for emotive words (e.g., *sonrisa*, *transformación*).
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures clear clinical legibility across all clinical parameters, appointment flows, and treatment plans. Its open counters and geometric warmth prevent cold, sterile detachment.
- **Microcopy & Data Labels:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold and bold weights with intentional positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) to ensure instant legibility for medical dosage, doctor accreditations, and schedule times.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a fluid 12-column structural grid for desktop and a 4-column grid for mobile, balanced by purposeful negative space to signal luxury and cleanliness.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1280px` centered canvas, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`. Generous vertical section padding (`space-xl` scaled to 5rem via layout blocks) conveys premium poise.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Multicolumn medical cards reflow into 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`. Complex treatment comparison tables transform into swipeable horizontal cards.

### Spacing Philosophy
Compact padding inside medical data sheets maintains high information density, while patient-facing landing sections expand with airy margins to deliver a relaxed, stress-free reading cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses luminous ambient layering rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing spotless clinical purity:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Recessed):** Background canvas (`#FCFDFD`) and muted clinical sub-containers (`#F8FAFC`). Flat with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card / Resting Surface):** Pure white cards with an ambient dual-layer shadow: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 8px 16px -2px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.04)` enclosed in a 1px border of `#F1F5F9`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating Module):** Raised cards, date pickers, and active booking slots: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Diagnostic Overlay):** Focus dialogs and smile consultation lightboxes: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`. Supported by a frosted medical glass backdrop (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a calibrated **Roundedness Level of 2** (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline radius):

- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** Standard radius of `0.5rem` (`8px`). Conveys contemporary precision without the austerity of sharp 90-degree corners.
- **Cards & Treatment Panels (`rounded-lg`):** Scaled to `1rem` (`16px`). Softens large surface boundaries and frames clinical before-and-after imagery gently.
- **Modals & Hero Focal Containers (`rounded-xl`):** Scaled to `1.5rem` (`24px`). Evokes relaxed, ergonomic organic contours.
- **Badges, Tags, and Pill Avatars:** Fully rounded (`9999px` radius) to signify touch-friendly interactive chips and medical status tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0D9488` with `#FFFFFF` label, `0.5rem` radius, bold label font, and 12px 24px padding. Hover: `#0F766E` with subtle upward transform (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary / Slate:** Deep `#0F172A` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for high-level consultations and aesthetic dentistry inquiries.
- **Outline / Clinical:** Transparent fill, 1.5px border in `#0D9488`, text in `#0D9488`. Hover: `#F0FDFA` fill.

### Cards
- **Clinical Treatment Card:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1rem` border-radius, 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, subtle teal ambient elevation. Features a high-resolution aspect-ratio header image, treatment tag, Playfair Display title, and clear action link.
- **Testimonial / Transformation Card:** Clean white card paired with an amber (`#D97706`) 5-star rating chip, verified patient badge, and dual before/after smile comparison slider.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** 48px height, `0.5rem` border-radius, `#FFFFFF` surface with `#CBD5E1` border. On focus: `#0D9488` border with a 3px ring of `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`. Text in `#0F172A`, placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- **Date & Slot Pickers:** Segmented chips; available slots in white with `#E2E8F0` border; selected slot in solid `#0D9488` with white text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Clinical Status Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), 6px 14px padding, `label-md` typography. 
  - *Active / Hygiene:* `#CCFBF1` background with `#0F766E` text.
  - *Aesthetic Exclusive:* `#FEF3C7` background with `#B45309` text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** `0.375rem` (checkbox) or circular (radio), 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border. Checked state transitions smoothly to solid `#0D9488` featuring a crisp white geometric check or inner disc.

### Specialized Dental Components
- **Smile Gallery Slider:** Interactive split before-and-after card with an ultra-thin teal divider line (`#0D9488`) and centered circular drag handle.
- **Doctor Credential Banner:** Compact doctor card highlighting specialty badges, certified license codes, and a direct booking trigger.
